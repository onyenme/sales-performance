# Project Overview
This Sales Analysis Report was developed for the purpose of  evaluating overall business performance through analyzing revenue, profitability, customer distribution, product contribution and seasonal sales trends.

The core objective was to transform transactional sales data into a structured performance intelligence which can support  strategic decisions across product, customer segment and time dimensions.


# Tool Used: 

i. Power BI

ii. Microsoft Excel

iii. SQL


# Focus: 
Revenue Performance, Customer Segmentation & Product Analysis


# Executive KPI Summary

a. Total Sales: 2.30M                

It indicates a strong revenue performance within the reporting period.

b. Total Customers: 10K

This demonstrates a broad customer base and that of a healthy market penetration.

c. Total Profit: 286.40K

It shows a positive profitability which needs a margin evaluation that is  related to total sales.

 d. Total Orders: 10K

Suggests an average of approximately 1 order per customer  while indicating a potential opportunity for repeating purchase growth.


# Detailed Performance Analysis

a. Sales by Category

i.	Technology: 0.84M

ii.	Furniture: 0.74M

iii.	Office Supplies: 0.72M

Key Insight:

Technology leads in revenue contribution which shows higher product value or stronger demand.

Strength:

A balanced category distribution reduces the tendency of revenue risk.

Weakness:

Furniture and Office Supplies slightly underperform compared to Technology which has a demand optimization opportunity.


b. Sales by Segment

i.	Consumer: 1.16M

ii.	Corporate: 0.71M

iii.	Home Office: 0.43M

Key Insight:

The Consumer segment dominates total sales which contributed more than 50% of revenue.

Strength:

It has a very strong retail market penetration.

Weakness:

i. Revenue concentration risk  has over-dependence on Consumer segment.

ii. Corporate and Home Office segments presented growth opportunities.


c. Monthly Sales Trend

Key observations:

i.	Lower performance in February (~0.06M)

ii.	Significant peak in September (~0.31M)

iii.	Strong Q4 performance (November ~0.35M, December ~0.33M)

The Insight :

The Sales demonstrated a seasonality with strong performance in Q3 and Q4.

Strength:

The end of the year sales spike suggested an effective seasonal demand capture.

Weakness:

The early year sales dip may indicate:

i.	Weak promotional strategy

ii.	Budget cycle constraints

iii.	Reduced demand post-holiday season

Opportunity exists only for Q1 campaign optimization.


d. Top 10 Products by Total Sales

Leading product:

i.	Canon imageCLASS 2200 (~62K)

Other strong performers:

ii.	Fellowes PB500 Electric Punch (~27K)

iii. Cisco TelePresence System (~23K)

iv.	HON 5400 Series Task Chairs (~22K)

The Insight:

Revenue concentration exists within a small group of high-performing products.

Strength:

There is a clear flagship products which drives sales performance.

Weakness:

Heavy reliance on top products increase risk exposure especially if the demand shifts or supply disruption tend to occur.

Portfolio diversification strategy may also improve stability.


e. Profitability Perspective

With the  total sales of 2.30M and profit of 286.40K. The estimated profit margin = 12–13%.

key Insight:

While revenue volume is strong, margin is moderate.

Strength:

Business is highly profitable.

Weakness:

Margin optimization opportunity through:

i.	Cost control

ii.	Pricing adjustments

iii. Supplier renegotiation

iv.	Product mix optimization
